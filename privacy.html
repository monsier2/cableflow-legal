CableFlow privacy policy

CableFlow is provided by Hanne. The app works without an account and does not send your project data to us. It has no in-app ads or analytics.

Data on your device

Your project names, row counts, chart and shaping rules, attached PDFs, and PDF reading positions are stored on your device. If you choose to open a PDF in an external viewer, that viewer can read it and handles the file under its own privacy practices.

Removing a PDF attachment and saving removes it from the project. Android may cache files temporarily while you select them. Clear CableFlow’s app storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to delete all locally saved projects and attachments. Your original files remain where you saved them.

Contact and support

Email support@hannelife.com for privacy questions or requests. If you contact us, we use the information you choose to send to respond and keep it as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations.
